01 — Print

Brochures, flyers & certificates

Brochure

Embassy Summer — 2027 brochure

This brochure was designed to be colourful, playful and energetic, reflecting the vibrance and fun of the Embassy Summer offering — an international summer school experience that combines language learning with fun activities.

With a wide range of destinations featured, I introduced colour coding along the outer edge of the pages, allowing readers to quickly recognise and flip to the destination they are interested in when browsing the printed brochure.

I also deliberately chose destination imagery with an unexpected twist rather than relying on the most obvious landmarks. For example, instead of using the typical Valletta skyline, I chose a night-time image of Valletta with fireworks.

Brochure

Summer Academy for Future Leaders — 2027 brochure

This particular brochure was designed to reflect the academic nature of the brand and its programmes in London and Boston.

I selected imagery that communicates the experiences the brand wanted to highlight, including networking with peers from around the world, interacting with people from diverse backgrounds, collaborating in groups, presenting ideas and exploring new cities.

I integrated student testimonials throughout the brochure to add credibility.

I also created a student journey spread (pages: 10-11), guiding readers through the experience of studying with Global Achievers Academy from start to finish.
